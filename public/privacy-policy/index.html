Privacy Policy for Wonni

Last Updated: August 2024

Introduction

Wonni ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

Information We Collect

1. Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name and username
  • Profile photo
  • Authentication credentials (managed by Firebase Authentication)

2. Listing Information

When you create or manage listings, we collect and store:

  • Item title, description, price, and quantity
  • Item condition and category
  • Shipping information (weight, dimensions, handling time)
  • Photos and images you upload
  • Listing status (draft, published, sold)

3. Marketplace Integration Data

When you connect marketplace accounts (such as Mercari), we collect and store:

  • Marketplace authentication tokens and OAuth credentials
  • Your marketplace username and account connection status
  • Cross-posting status and linked listing IDs on marketplaces
  • Sales data synced from connected marketplaces

4. Sales and Transaction Data

We collect information about your sales, including:

  • Sales history and order details
  • Payment and earnings information
  • Shipping and fulfillment data

5. Device and Usage Information

We automatically collect certain information when you use the App:

  • Device type, operating system, and mobile device identifiers
  • App usage patterns and feature interactions
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Analytics data (via Firebase Analytics)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Wonni App and our services
  • To enable you to create, manage, and publish listings
  • To facilitate marketplace integrations and cross-posting
  • To track and display your sales and earnings
  • To authenticate your account and secure access
  • To communicate with you about your account and transactions
  • To analyze App usage and performance (analytics)
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To prevent fraud and maintain security

Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services and platforms. These services may collect and process your data according to their own privacy policies:

Firebase (Google)

We use Firebase for:

  • User authentication and account management
  • Cloud data storage (Firestore)
  • File storage (Firebase Storage)
  • Analytics and crash reporting

Firebase/Google Privacy Policy

Mercari

When you connect your Mercari account, we collect OAuth tokens and sync sales data with Mercari's API.

Mercari Privacy Policy

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure. We implement security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication via Firebase
  • Access controls and firewall rules
  • Regular security updates and monitoring

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Marketplace Services: We share necessary data with Mercari and other connected marketplaces to facilitate listings and sales
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or government request
  • Service Providers: We share data with service providers who assist us (e.g., Firebase for storage)
  • Business Transfers: If Wonni is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction

Your Rights and Controls

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: You can access your data by logging into your Wonni account
  • Correction: You can edit your profile and listing information in the App
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us
  • Opt-out: You can disable analytics and crash reporting in your device settings

Children's Privacy

Wonni is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

Retention of Data

We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above and posting the revised policy in the App. Your continued use of the App following such notification constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
